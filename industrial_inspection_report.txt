SOVEREIGN INDUSTRIAL INSPECTION REPORT
Report ID: IR-2024-0042
Date: 2024-10-09
Inspector: J. Martinez
Facility: Northgate Processing Plant - Building A

1.0 EQUIPMENT OVERVIEW
This report covers the inspection of Pump P-101, a centrifugal pump
manufactured by Grundfos (Model NK 80-200). The pump has been in
continuous operation since installation on 2019-03-15. The pump is
located in Building A, Section 3, and is part of the primary cooling
loop. The serial number is SN-2019-0315-A.

2.0 OPERATING PARAMETERS
- Maximum operating pressure: 150 PSI
- Normal operating pressure: 120 PSI
- Maximum flow rate: 500 GPM
- Operating temperature: 65°C
- Rated speed: 1750 RPM
- Motor power: 25 kW

3.0 MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
The pump P-101 requires maintenance every 6 months. The last
maintenance was performed on 2024-04-15. The next scheduled
maintenance is due on 2024-10-15. Maintenance includes bearing
inspection, seal replacement, and impeller cleaning.

4.0 INSPECTION FINDINGS

4.1 Bearing Condition
The bearing on pump P-101 shows signs of wear. Vibration analysis
indicates a 15% increase from baseline readings. The bearing
temperature was measured at 78°C, which is above the recommended
maximum of 70°C. This is a critical finding that requires immediate
attention. Bearing replacement is recommended within 7 days.

4.2 Seal Integrity
The mechanical seal shows minor leakage. The leak rate was measured
at 2 drops per minute, which is within acceptable limits but should
be monitored. Seal replacement is recommended during the next
scheduled maintenance.

4.3 Impeller Condition
The impeller shows moderate corrosion on the leading edges. This is
consistent with the pump age (5 years). No immediate action is
required, but the impeller should be replaced within the next 12
months to prevent performance degradation.

4.4 Discharge Pressure
The discharge pressure was measured at 118 PSI, which is within
the normal operating range of 120 PSI. No issues detected.

5.0 RISK ASSESSMENT
The bearing wear is classified as a CRITICAL risk. If the bearing
fails, the pump will seize, potentially causing a catastrophic
failure of the cooling loop. This could lead to overheating of
downstream equipment and a potential fire hazard. Immediate action
is required to prevent a safety incident.

The seal leakage is classified as a LOW risk. The current leak rate
is manageable, but if it increases, it could lead to environmental
contamination.

The impeller corrosion is classified as a MEDIUM risk. While not
immediate, it will affect pump efficiency over time.

6.0 RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Schedule emergency bearing replacement within 7 days - CRITICAL
2. Replace mechanical seal during next maintenance window - HIGH
3. Plan impeller replacement within 12 months - MEDIUM
4. Increase vibration monitoring to daily until bearing replaced
5. Notify safety officer of the critical bearing condition
6. Prepare contingency plan for cooling loop shutdown

7.0 CONCLUSION
Pump P-101 is currently operational but has a critical bearing
condition that requires immediate attention. With prompt action,
the risk of catastrophic failure can be mitigated. The pump should
remain in service with enhanced monitoring until the bearing is
replaced.

End of Report.
